[ 03 ] The two lists

Every deal is on one list.
Never both.

The line is sixty days since a real conversation. On one side, the deals still moving. On the other, the ones that went quiet and nobody opened again.

Pipeline

The deals still in play

It reads every open deal and flags the ones about to go quiet.

Reply gaps growing. Proposals aging past what your team normally takes. Deals stuck in a stage longer than 90% of the ones that actually closed.

Each one comes with the next move, written from that deal’s own history, ranked so your rep starts at the top instead of scrolling.

WinBack

The deals everyone stopped opening

Everything past the line. Including the ones nobody ever worked in the first place.

It starts with why the deal stalled. That reason is on the record, pulled from the notes, the transcripts and the whole email thread, so the first email opens on the real objection instead of pretending the last six months didn’t happen.

Then it builds a sequence around it. Each email does one job and asks for the same thing. Get back on the calendar.

And it sharpens without anyone running a test. Every send is scored on what came back.

Which hook got it opened. Which subject got the reply. Which length, which angle, which case study.

What works becomes what it writes next.

Your rep reads it and sends it. Nothing goes out on its own.

A deal is only ever on one of them. The day they reply, it moves back.
