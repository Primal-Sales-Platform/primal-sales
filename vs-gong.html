The five things you're thinking.

"We already have Gong. Why add this?"
Because they do different jobs. Gong is your system of record for conversations. PrimalSales.ai is your system of action — it extracts the pattern out of your won calls and coaches it live, on the next call. If you're honestly getting coaching ROI from your recorder today, you don't need us. Most teams aren't, which is the whole reason this exists.
"Do we have to rip out Gong to use this?"
No. We connect to the CRM you already run — HubSpot or GoHighLevel — and read your closed-won calls from there. Nothing is migrated and nothing is switched off. Teams that later drop their recorder do it because they stopped opening it, not because we asked them to.
"How does the price actually compare?"
Ours is on the site: $1,997/mo for three seats, $497 per additional seat. Gong is quote-only with seat minimums and an annual commitment; published median contracts land around $54,900 a year. And the shapes differ — a per-seat recorder gets more expensive every time you hire a closer. A per-team price doesn't.
"Is this really built for a team our size?"
Three to ten closers on HubSpot or GoHighLevel, run by someone who's also doing five other jobs. That's the whole target. Enterprise platforms assume a RevOps team to configure and maintain them, and you don't have one. Fewer than three closers and we're probably too early for you — the spread between your best and worst rep isn't there yet.
"We run everything in Salesforce."
Then we're early for you. HubSpot and GoHighLevel are live today; Salesforce is on the roadmap and not shipped. We'd rather tell you that now than on install day.
