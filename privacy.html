Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Primal Sales AI, LLC ("Primal Sales," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Primal Sales platform and related services (the "Service"), available at primalsales.ai. By using the Service, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account & organization data: name, email address, role, company/organization name, and login credentials when you or your administrator create an account.
  • Content you submit: sales call transcripts, notes, methodology and playbook configuration, email and message drafts, and other content you or your team upload or generate in the Service.
  • Billing data: subscription and payment information, processed by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not store full card numbers.

Information from connected services

When you connect a third‑party account (such as Google, a CRM like HubSpot/GoHighLevel/Salesforce, a call‑recording provider, or a messaging provider), we receive data from those services as authorized by you to provide the Service's features.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: log data, device and browser information, and feature interactions used to operate, secure, and improve the Service.
  • Cookies: we use strictly necessary and functional cookies to keep you signed in and remember preferences.

2. Google User Data and Limited Use

If you choose to connect your Google account, you grant Primal Sales access to specific Google data through Google OAuth. We request the minimum scopes needed for the features you enable:

Google data / scopeWhy we access it
Basic profile & email (sign‑in)To authenticate you and link your Google identity to your Primal Sales account.
Google Calendar (events)To sync your upcoming calls/meetings into the Service so reps see their schedule, prep, and post‑call workflows.
Gmail — send on your behalf (gmail.send)To send sales emails (reminders, confirmations, follow‑ups, win‑back outreach) from your own connected mailbox when you trigger or approve them. We do not request read access to your inbox for this feature.
Limited Use. Primal Sales' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:
  • We only use Google user data to provide and improve the user‑facing features described above.
  • We do not transfer or sell Google user data to third parties for advertising, marketing, or other unrelated purposes.
  • We do not use Google user data for personalized or interest‑based advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless: (a) you give explicit consent for specific data; (b) it is necessary for security purposes (e.g., investigating abuse); (c) it is required to comply with applicable law; or (d) the data is aggregated/anonymized and used for internal operations.

3. AI/ML and Your Data

Primal Sales uses third‑party AI models (including Anthropic's Claude) to generate coaching, analysis, drafts, and summaries from content you provide.

We do not use Google Workspace APIs data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non‑personalized AI and/or machine‑learning models. Google user data is used only to deliver the features you have enabled, and is not used as training data for AI/ML models. Our AI providers process content to return results to you and do not train their foundation models on your data through our enterprise/API configuration.

4. How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, secure, and maintain the Service and its features.
  • Generate AI coaching, call analysis, recommendations, and drafts you request.
  • Send transactional and feature‑related communications (e.g., account, billing, and the sales emails you trigger/approve from your connected mailbox).
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • Detect, prevent, and address security incidents, fraud, and abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate the Service:

  • Service providers (subprocessors) that help us run the Service, under contractual confidentiality and data‑protection obligations, including: Supabase (database/hosting), Vercel (application hosting), Anthropic (AI processing), Stripe (payments), and the third‑party integrations you choose to connect (e.g., your CRM, email, calendar, and recording providers).
  • Within your organization: content and analytics are visible to authorized members of your own organization (e.g., your managers/admins), consistent with your team's configuration.
  • Legal & safety: when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the Service.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

Data is isolated per organization (multi‑tenant separation), and one customer's data is not shared with another customer.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. You can disconnect a connected account (including Google) at any time from your settings, which revokes our ongoing access to that account's data.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data. Upon account termination or a verified deletion request, customer account data is deleted within 30 days, after which it is permanently removed from active systems (backups age out on our standard cycle). You can also revoke Primal Sales' access to your Google Account at any time via Google Account permissions.

7. Security

  • OAuth tokens and other credentials are encrypted at rest.
  • Data access is scoped per organization and protected by row‑level security and authenticated, server‑side access controls.
  • Transport is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS).

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry‑standard safeguards.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
