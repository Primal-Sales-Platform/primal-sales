The four questions we get about the price.

"Why isn't the founding rate on the page?"
Because it's set against what the extraction actually finds in your pipeline, and I'd rather put a number next to your numbers than post one that means nothing on its own. The list price is right there at the top so you're not walking into a call blind — the founding rate is lower than it, on the same three seats.
"Is there an annual plan or a discount for paying up front?"
No. Everything here is monthly and there's nothing to sign. If it stops earning its place, you leave. Locking you into a year is how software stops having to be good.
"What happens when we go over 300 calls?"
$0.99 per call after that, on the same month's bill. No cutoff, no throttle, no upgrade conversation. Most teams of three to ten closers don't reach it; the ones that do are usually happy about why.
"What if we add closers?"
$497 a month per additional seat. That's the whole change — the platform price doesn't re-quote because your floor grew, which is the part that gets expensive on a per-seat enterprise contract.
