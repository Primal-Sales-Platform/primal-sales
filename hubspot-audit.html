[ THE PART WORTH THINKING ABOUT ]

Your CRM is keeping two records, not one.

The fair objection is that if nobody keeps the CRM current, nobody can get a real number out of it either. That's the right question, and it's the whole thing.

The first record is what people typed. Notes, summaries, whether anyone logged the call, whether a reason ever got picked from a dropdown. That's the one your reports are built on. It's opinion, and most of it is missing.

The second is what the system wrote down on its own. When a deal was created. When it moved. When it stopped moving. Whether anything ever actually happened on it.

Nobody types a timestamp.

Nobody can massage one.

A deal your rep worked and lost, and a deal nobody ever called, look identical on your dashboard and nothing alike underneath. One has activity on it. The other has silence.

The silence is the part that can't lie to you.
