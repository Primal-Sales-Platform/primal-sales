[ 05 ] The details

Questions, answered.

Do I have to be a Primal customer to refer? +
No. Anyone can be a partner. If you are a customer, even better — you can speak from experience.
What counts as a referral? +
Someone who signs up through your unique link and becomes a paying customer. Clicks and sign-ups show up in your portal along the way, so you can watch interest build before anyone converts.
How and when do I get paid? +
Commission accrues each month your referred account pays us, and payouts go out monthly through our payout partner once approved. Everything is itemized in your portal.
How long does the commission last? +
It's recurring for the life of the account — you keep earning every month your referral stays a customer, per your partner terms.
How do I track my referrals and earnings? +
Your partner portal shows every link click, referral, sign-up, and payout in real time — plus ready-to-send intro templates and a who-to-refer cheat sheet.
How fast is approval? +
We review each application by hand so we only bring on partners we can support. You'll hear back by email with your referral link and portal access.
