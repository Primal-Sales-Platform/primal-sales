[ HOW IT WORKS ]

Patterns, not recordings.

Three capabilities turn your closed-won calls into something the whole team can run. All read from the same extracted playbook.

Methodology Extraction
Point it at 10 closed-won deals and it answers the question your recorder never could: why did these close? The discovery sequence, the multi-threading move, the exact language your top AEs use — written out and teachable.
  • The "why we win," surfaced from your own wins
  • Win-rate deltas when the pattern is applied
  • A board-ready playbook, updated as deals close
Closed-Loop Learning
Every won deal triggers deep analysis. Every lost deal too. New patterns feed back into the playbook automatically, so the system gets sharper every week — on your data, not a vendor's aggregate of everyone else's.
  • Auto-analysis on every closed deal
  • Playbook updates as new patterns emerge
  • Win/loss intelligence enablement can actually act on
Live Call Companion + Ace
The companion rides every live call and surfaces the right reframe in real time. Ace, the per-rep AI coach, remembers each AE's calls and pipeline and pushes them on the exact gaps between them and your top performer.
  • Objection reframes + buying-signal read, live
  • Ace coaches each rep on their own calls, persistently
  • Roleplay against personas built from your real prospects
