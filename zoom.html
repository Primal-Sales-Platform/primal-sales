[ 01 ] Zoom Integration

What the Zoom integration does

In one line: PrimalSales reads the transcripts Zoom already makes for your cloud recordings, scores each sales call against your own sales process, and turns that into coaching for the rep who made the call.

Sales calls are the richest record a company has of why deals close and why they don't, and almost nobody reviews them. A manager who wants to coach one rep has to sit through hours of recording to find the ten minutes that mattered. PrimalSales reads the transcript instead.

What it reads

  • The transcript of each cloud recording on the connected account
  • The meeting topic, start time and duration, so a call can be matched to the right deal and the right person
  • The connected user's own name and email, so calls are attributed to the correct rep

What it never does

  • It never joins your meetings. No bot appears in the participant list.
  • It never records anything. It reads recordings Zoom already made because you asked Zoom to make them.
  • It never downloads audio or video. Only the text transcript.
  • It never creates, changes or deletes anything in Zoom. The connection is read-only, and that is enforced in our code rather than left as a promise.
[ 02 ] Zoom Integration

Before you connect

The one thing that catches most people: Zoom only produces a transcript if both cloud recording and audio transcription are switched on. If transcription is off, PrimalSales connects successfully and then finds nothing to read — because there is nothing there.

Check these in Zoom first

  1. Sign in at zoom.us → Settings → Recording.
  2. Cloud recording must be ON. Local recordings stay on the recorder's own computer and Zoom's API cannot see them, so PrimalSales cannot either.
  3. Under cloud recording, Create audio transcript must be ON.
  4. If your Zoom account is managed by an admin, these may be locked at the account level — an admin has to switch them on.

Who should connect

Each rep connects their own Zoom account, so their own recordings are attributed to them. A manager connecting on a rep's behalf will have those calls attributed to the manager.

What you need in PrimalSales

An admin or manager role on your PrimalSales organisation. Closers can view their own scored calls but do not connect the recorder.

[ 03 ] Zoom Integration

Connecting and using it

Connect

  1. Sign in to PrimalSales.
  2. Go to Admin → Transcription.
  3. Choose Zoom as your call recorder.
  4. Click Connect Zoom. You are taken to Zoom's own consent screen, which lists exactly what you are approving.
  5. Approve. You are returned to PrimalSales and the panel shows Connected.

If a permission is missing: the panel tells you which one, by name. Zoom does not reject a permission it does not recognise — it quietly grants the ones it knows and drops the rest — so PrimalSales compares what Zoom granted against what was asked for and shows you the difference rather than reporting a healthy connection that cannot read anything.

What happens next

PrimalSales checks for new cloud recordings every 15 minutes. A transcript usually appears in Zoom a few minutes after a call ends, and in PrimalSales shortly after that. On first connect you can also pull in recent history so there is something to look at on day one.

Where the calls appear

  • Call Library — every analysed call, searchable.
  • Dashboard — scores and trends per rep.
  • Coaching — the specific moments worth working on, per rep.
[ 04 ] Zoom Integration

Disconnecting

You can remove the connection from either side, and either one stops it.

From PrimalSales

  1. Admin → Transcription → Zoom.
  2. Click Disconnect.
  3. The stored authorisation is deleted immediately. No further recordings are read.

From Zoom

  1. Sign in at zoom.us → Marketplace → Manage → Added Apps.
  2. Find Primal Sales Platform and click Remove.

What happens to calls already analysed: they stay in your PrimalSales account, because they are your team's coaching history. To delete them as well, delete the calls in Call Library, or email support@primalsales.ai and we will remove all Zoom-sourced data for your organisation.

[ 05 ] Zoom Integration

What we access, and why

These are the permissions the consent screen asks for. Every one of them is used; none is requested "just in case."

Permissions

  • View your recordings — to see that a call happened. Without this the integration has nothing to work from.
  • View your recording files — to find the transcript among the audio and video files attached to a recording.
  • View meeting transcripts — the transcript itself. This is the only file we download.
  • View a recording — to resolve one specific call rather than list many.
  • View meeting information — topic, start time, duration, so a call reaches the right deal.
  • View your user profile — your name and email, so calls are attributed to you.

How it is stored

  • Your Zoom authorisation is encrypted at rest and deleted the moment you disconnect.
  • Transcripts are stored inside your own organisation and are visible only to members of it.
  • Transcript text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API for analysis. It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else.

Full detail is in our Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum.

Troubleshooting & support

It says Connected but no calls appear

Almost always one of three things:

  1. Audio transcription is off in Zoom. Cloud recording alone produces no transcript. See Before you connect.
  2. The transcript has not generated yet. Zoom takes a few minutes after the call ends. Check the recording in Zoom — if there is no transcript file there, there is nothing for us to read yet.
  3. The recordings are local, not cloud. Local recordings never leave the recorder's computer.

A permission is reported as missing

Open the Zoom app's permissions, add the named permission, and click Re-authorize in PrimalSales. Re-authorising replaces the existing connection; you do not need to disconnect first.

The connection stopped working

A Zoom authorisation can be revoked from the Zoom side, or expire if unused for a long period. Click Re-authorize in Admin → Transcription → Zoom.

Calls are attributed to the wrong person

Calls are attributed to the Zoom account that recorded them. If a manager connected on a rep's behalf, disconnect and have the rep connect their own account.

Support

Email support@primalsales.ai. Include your organisation name and, if a specific call is missing, the meeting topic and the date. We answer within one business day.
