[ 02 ] Why four calls

One winning call tells you
almost nothing.

Read a single call that closed and every line in it looks like the reason. The rep was warm, asked good questions, handled the price. So were the calls that didn’t close.

The only way to know what actually moved the deal is to see what shows up in the wins and is missing from the losses. That takes both piles. Two and two is the smallest honest sample — below it, we’d be handing you a summary with a confident voice.
