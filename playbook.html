[ 02 ] Why four calls

Start with four calls.
Look for useful differences.

A winning call can teach you something. Comparing it with calls from lost deals gives that lesson more context: which questions were asked, how buyers responded, and where the conversations changed.

Two won and two lost deals are a practical starting point, not proof of what caused a sale. Choose calls from similar stages and offers. Treat the first-pass patterns as coaching hypotheses to test against more calls.
