FROM THE FOUNDER

"I built this because I lived the problem for 25 years."

Twenty-five years in direct response. Fifteen years building persuasion methodologies used by teams closing nine and ten figures. Three years inside one of the fastest-growing agencies in the U.S. — sitting through hundreds of recorded calls and realizing existing coaching tools couldn't tell me what my top closers were actually doing differently.

PrimalSales.ai is the platform I wish existed when I was running sales. Built by a closer for closers — not by an AI team writing for a market they've never sold into.

WHY "PRIMAL"

All those years of persuasion and influence work taught me one thing above everything else: people don't decide with logic. They decide with emotion, in the gut, before the words ever reach the head. Then they justify it with logic afterward. The YES doesn't come from a script. It comes from a feeling one human creates in another.

That's why no AI will ever close the way a great salesperson does, and it's why Primal is built the opposite way from everyone else. We don't put AI between your rep and the prospect, feeding them lines to read. We put AI behind your rep: training them, drilling them, coaching them on their own calls, until the human connection is theirs. The AI helps most while they're learning, and fades as they get great.

Sales is primal. The AI's job isn't to replace the human. It's to enhance the human.

— Jared Emin, Founder

  • Learns across 50+ signals every time it's used, sharpening what actually gets a stalled prospect back on the calendar
  • The moment they respond or rebook, the deal exits WinBack and lands back in the closer's active pipeline to manage — never sitting in two places at once
